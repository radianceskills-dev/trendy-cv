# Future Improvements

## Reassess the AI-generated section plan

Deferred by user decision. Do not include this option in the current workflow.

The initial target-based AI plan may omit a section needed by information supplied later, such as publications or professional licenses.

Potential future workflow:
- Preserve unmatched parsed information in an unassigned review area outside the rendered CV.
- Offer an explicit “Reassess section plan” action.
- AI proposes a revised plan using approved section types and preserves existing entries.
- Apply the revised plan only after user confirmation.

Current scope: users populate the fixed planned sections and may add/edit/remove entries within them. Users cannot add sections. Empty sections are excluded from the final CV.
